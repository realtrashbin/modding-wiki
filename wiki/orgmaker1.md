# History
The history of Orgmaker 1 or Orgmaker V1.3.4 begins in 1999, Pixel wanted to make an improved version of PiyoPiyo; his previous musical software. Pixel then proceeded to spend about 2 years working on orgmaker, updating it until 2001.
Pixel also had presumably his friends test the software and also making some music in Organya(Orgmaker's unique and only format).

# Usage
## Cave Story Modding
Orgmaker can be used for a variety of uses, an example would be for Cave Story because Cave Story's music format is Organya.
## Outside of Cave Story
With the invent of extensions of organya like organya.h and Org001.dll using Organya outside of Organya native games has never been easier!
